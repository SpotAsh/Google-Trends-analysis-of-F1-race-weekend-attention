3. OUTLIER DETECTION
========================================================================

DEFINITION: an outlier is a race weekend whose attention profile (5 standardized features)
is unusual compared with the other races: far from its neighbours in feature space, or
extreme on one feature. It can be (a) a rare but valid event, (b) an artifact of Google
Trends (an empty or zero baseline, an ambiguous search term), or (c) a genuinely
different kind of race weekend.

Methods and settings (contamination / nu / percentile = 5% for the model-based methods):
  z>3            : any feature with |z| > 3 (statistical threshold, univariate)
  IsolationForest: 300 trees, contamination 0.05 (random-partition isolation)
  LOF            : 15 neighbours, contamination 0.05 (local density vs neighbours)
  kNN distance   : mean distance to the 5 nearest neighbours, top 5% flagged
  OneClassSVM    : RBF kernel, gamma='scale', nu 0.05 (boundary around the bulk of the data)
